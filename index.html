Your exact path: debit card to first trade

This is the complete beginner journey. Assume you have a debit card, a computer, and no crypto knowledge. Follow these in order.

  1. 1
    Become a member

    Join the membership on this page. This unlocks downloads and support.

  2. 2
    Download and install the app

    Download KnightTrader BloFin for Windows 11 or Mac. Install it and open it. Sign in with your membership email and password.

  3. 3
    Install ProtonVPN and get an allowed country

    BloFin may block some regions. Use the free ProtonVPN routine in the How-To tab to reach Romania, Poland, Netherlands, or Mexico.

  4. 4
    Create your free Nous Portal API key

    Open the Nous Portal, choose the free plan, and create an API key. Paste it into the app under Settings.

  5. 5
    Create a Blofin account and API keys

    Sign up for Blofin, create API keys with the required permissions, and save them in the app.

  6. 6
    Create a Coinbase account and buy crypto with your debit card

    If you do not have crypto already, sign up for Coinbase, verify your ID, add your debit card, buy a supported crypto, and send it to Blofin.

  7. 7
    Install Hermes and start the dashboard

    Use the Hermes tab in the app to install Hermes, write the credentials file, and start the local dashboard.

  8. 8
    Auto-configure the trading cron

    Click Auto-Configure Cron. After that, Hermes trades on Blofin on the schedule while your computer stays awake.

FAQ

I am new to crypto. Is this for me?

Yes. The app includes a How-To tab that explains each step. You can follow along even if you have never used a trading bot or AI assistant before.

I only have a debit card. Can I still do this?

Yes. Use Coinbase to buy crypto with your debit card, then send it to Blofin. The How-To tab includes exact steps.

Do I need a VPN?

BloFin restricts some jurisdictions, so a VPN may be required for platform access from your region. The How-To tab includes a free ProtonVPN routine.

Do I have to place trades myself?

No. After setup, the assistant can trade on your behalf. You can inspect logs and the dashboard anytime.

Where do my API keys go?

They stay on your machine. The app stores credentials locally and syncs them into your local Hermes setup.

What if I need help?

Open the How-To tab inside the app. It walks through ProtonVPN, Coinbase, Blofin, Hermes setup, API keys, and keeping your computer awake.

Is this a subscription or hosted service?

No. It is a local app with guided setup. There is no ongoing account with us beyond the membership and the crypto exchange accounts you control.

What should I do if the download button breaks?

Sign in first, then try again. If it still breaks, contact support through your membership channel.
