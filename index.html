What it actually does, in plain English

KnightTrader BloFin is a desktop app for Windows 11 and macOS 11+ that runs a small AI trading assistant on your computer. That assistant looks at your BloFin account, decides whether to place or close a trade, and acts on a schedule. You watch the dashboard. You can stop it any time.

1

You fund a BloFin account

You open a BloFin account (the app links you straight to the signup page), buy a small amount of crypto with your debit card on Coinbase, and send it to BloFin. That is your trading money.

2

You create a BloFin API key and Nous Portal key

An API key is a long password that lets the app place trades on your behalf without you logging in each time. The How-To tab shows the exact screen and exactly which boxes to tick. You also create a free Nous Portal API key ($5 credits) for the AI model calls.

3

You install the app

Select your operating system above, download the one-click installer, and run it. No terminal, no Python, no PATH edits.

4

The assistant runs on a schedule

Every few minutes the assistant checks your account and the market, asks its AI model whether to act, and either does nothing or places a small order. Every decision is logged locally on your PC.

5

You keep the kill switch

You can hit "Stop" in the app, or close the app, or uninstall it. The bot stops within seconds. Your money never leaves BloFin — only the API key on your PC is revoked.

Questions beginners actually ask

Does this work on a Mac?

Yes. KnightTrader BloFin ships a signed .dmg for macOS 11 (Big Sur) and newer on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. Open the .dmg, drag the app into Applications, and the How-To tab handles the rest. The ProtonVPN helper inside the app is Windows-only — Mac users can still run ProtonVPN manually, and the How-To tab shows how.

I have never bought crypto before. Can I still use this?

Yes — that is exactly who this is built for. The How-To tab starts with how to open a Coinbase account, how to buy USDC with your debit card, and how to send it to BloFin. You do it once and the app remembers it.

I only have a debit card. Can I still fund this?

Yes. You can use Coinbase to buy USDC with your debit card, then withdraw it to your BloFin account. The How-To tab includes exact screenshots. Some banks block crypto purchases — if yours does, the How-To tab suggests workarounds.

Do I need a VPN?

Maybe. BloFin restricts some jurisdictions, so depending on where you live, you may need a VPN to reach the BloFin website. The app itself runs locally. The How-To tab includes a free ProtonVPN routine if you need one.

Do I have to place trades myself?

No. After setup, the assistant places and closes trades on its schedule. You can open the app at any time to see open positions, recent activity, and logs. You can also stop the bot from the app — it does not need to be running for you to close trades manually on BloFin.

Where do my API keys go?

They stay on your machine. The app stores them in your local user profile and writes them into the Hermes sandbox folder when you click Install. They are never sent to anyone or uploaded anywhere.
